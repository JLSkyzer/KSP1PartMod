# Changelog

## 0.12.0 - 2026-10-28

Science: the KSP1 experiment modules that Redux dropped are imported as KSP2's own science module. Not run in game yet; checklist (French) in `docs/testing/2026-10-28-science-in-game-checklist.md`. Native science only: no lab, no reset, no diminishing returns, no biome table, no new module type.

- **Switches** (`animations.json`, applied by Redux's import whatever `enabled` is, except the two animation switches): `ksp1_science`, `science_generate_definitions`, `science_definitions_via_patch_manager`, `science_report_storage`, `science_transmit_split`, `science_deploy_animations`, `science_wait_for_animation` (default true), `science_value_scale` (default 1, 0.01 to 100), `science_native_ids`, `science_fallback_ids`, `science_skip_ids` (tables). `ksp1_science: false` gives the behaviour of 0.11.0.
- **Module.** `ModuleScienceExperiment`, `DMModuleScienceAnimate` and `DMModuleScienceAnimateGeneric` become `Module_ScienceExperiment`, the module of KSP2's crewed pods: every experiment module of a part is one `ExperimentConfiguration` of a single `Data_ScienceExperiment`, run from the part menu, an action group or the science button. A KSP1 id repeated on a part is merged; a KSP2 id reached by two KSP1 ids is kept once (they score once per location together, noted). Crew comes from KSP1's usage mask.
- **Three resolutions.** An identical KSP2 experiment when there is one (`science_native_ids`: `crewReport` -> `CrewObservation`); otherwise a generated definition `KSP1_<id>` built from the KSP1 `EXPERIMENT_DEFINITION` (title, situations, regions, value, data size, flavor texts); with a definition that is not generated, the closest KSP2 experiment to its situations (Jaccard; EVA-only experiments and `CrewReport` excluded); with no KSP1 definition on disk, the KSP2 experiment of `science_fallback_ids` (the stock instruments). The last two count as "closest" and are noted. Installed set, raw cfgs, with the stock `ScienceDefs.cfg`: 73 definitions read, 286 parts with the module, resolutions 310 generated, 48 identical, 0 closest, 18 skipped; without it: 55 definitions, 269 generated, 48 identical, 41 closest. A DMagic module whose `sitMask` or `bioMask` differs from its definition's gets its own definition `KSP1_<id>_s<sit>_b<bio>` (3 installed).
- **Generated definitions through Patch Manager.** Each one is a Patch Manager duplicate of `EnvironmentSurvey` whose data is replaced by the KSP1 definition, queued at pre-initialization in the mod's own scope, so it follows the campaign feature: a campaign where KSP1PartMod is off has no `KSP1_*` experiment. Their names and flavor texts go through Redux's localizer (one CSV of terms). `science_definitions_via_patch_manager: false` (or Patch Manager's C# science patching missing) adds them to the game's experiment store instead, only when the mod is on in the campaign. A check after the science data step logs how many are present (`science: store: p of g`) and any difference of KSP2's own experiments from 26w42c. The definitions are read whenever `ksp1_science` is on, even with generation off: the closest rule needs their situations.
- **Value split.** KSP1's `baseValue` times `science_value_scale` is the KSP2 value. An experiment with `xmitDataScalar` below 1 gives a data report worth that share, to transmit, and a sample worth the rest, to recover; recovering both gives KSP1's full value (`science_transmit_split: false`: one data report at full value). KSP2 multiplies by its own body, situation and region scalars, once per experiment, location and report type: the balance against KSP2's experiments is only checked in game.
- **Situations and regions in place of biomes.** KSP1's situation mask gives KSP2's situations (flying low and high are one KSP2 atmosphere situation, noted); a biome bit becomes "with region" on its situation. KSP1 result texts per body and situation are kept as flavor; per-biome texts are skipped.
- **Report storage.** A KSP1 part with a `ModuleScienceContainer` or an experiment gets `HasReportStorage`, as KSP2's command parts have: Redux sets it on no KSP1 part, so a vessel of KSP1 parts only could store no report at all (`science_report_storage`). Installed: 312 parts (74 with a container). A KSP1 instrument on a KSP2 craft gives it storage too; KSP2's storage has no capacity.
- **Clips and the wait gate.** KSP2 waits for a native deploy animation on any part with an `Animator`, forever on a KSP1 part; the mod clears that gate on the parts it converts. Running an experiment opens the clip KSP1 played: the stock module's `FxModules` (Toggle, Deploy or Redux follower entry) or the 2b `ScienceToggle` entry of a DMagic module (`science_deploy_animations`). A DMagic experiment with `experimentWaitForAnimation` completes once its clip has reached open, through KSP2's own deploy gate (`science_wait_for_animation`). Installed: 110 of 358 configurations open a clip, 95 wait. No animation entry is added and no saved key moves (tested on the stock instruments, a pod and four BDB parts with every switch on and off). One Unity warning `Parameter '' does not exist` can still show when a vessel loads with an experiment running: that trigger fires before any patch.
- **Saves.** A vessel whose saved experiments name a `KSP1_*` id that the store lacks (mod off, generation off) loses those experiments at load, with one line each (`science: <part>: <id> not in the experiment store, dropped`), instead of breaking the part. A vessel saved again there loses them for good.
- **Skipped, and why.** Labs and data processing (`ModuleScienceLab`, `ModuleScienceConverter`): KSP2 has no lab. Reset (`resettable`, `resettableOnEVA`, `resourceToReset`) and `rerunnable`: KSP2 has no reset. Diminishing returns and `scienceCap`: a KSP2 report scores once per experiment, location and type. Part tests (`ModuleTestSubject`): KSP2 missions test no parts. Breaking Ground ground science and the ROC scanner (`ROCScience`): no such system. KSP1's kerbal experiments (`evaReport`, `evaScience`, `surfaceSample`): KSP2 kerbals run their own. The specialised DMagic modules (`DMSIGINT`, `DMSoilMoisture`, `DMSeismicHammer`...): their behaviour is not readable from cfg fields. Also `keepDeployedMode`, `requireAtmosphere`/`requireNoAtmosphere`, DMagic `planetaryMask`, `excludeAtmosphere`, `asteroidReports`, `requiredParts`, `experimentsLimit`, `scienceValueRatio`, `totalScienceLevel`. Each is counted in the `native: science:` line.
- **Stock `ScienceDefs.cfg`.** A copy of the stock KSP1 part folders lacks `GameData/Squad/Resources/ScienceDefs.cfg`. Without it the stock instruments take the closest KSP2 experiment and several share one. Copy it to `mods/KSP1/Squad/Resources/ScienceDefs.cfg` yourself; nothing of it is shipped.
- **Audit.** The `native_modules` CSV column carries `Module_ScienceExperiment` and `report storage` (tagged `:missing` when the part definition lost the flag); the `audit: native modules:` line counts `<n> science part(s)`.
- **Safety.** Each importer failure leaves the part as 0.11.0 imports it, `HasReportStorage` included. A missing game member turns its feature off with one line. A runtime throw stops the science runtime patches for the session (`science: patch failed`).
- **Clean room.** No KSP1, Squad or DMagic code, and no KSP1 flavor text in the repository: the texts are read from your own `mods/KSP1` at load. Redux is called through its own members, resolved by name, never copied.
- **Radiators (two minors of 0.11.0).** A deployable radiator for which Redux keeps its own `Data_Deployable` logs it once (`thermal: <part>: Module_ActiveRadiator keeps Redux's own Data_Deployable (no deploy bridge)`). The radiator checklist notes that a radiator already deployed in a 0.10.0 save starts off.
- 2311 EditMode tests (2309 passing, 2 explicit and skipped).

## 0.11.0 - 2026-10-28

Radiators: the KSP1 `ModuleActiveRadiator` that Redux dropped is imported as KSP2's own cooler. Not run in game yet; checklist (French) in `docs/testing/2026-10-28-radiators-in-game-checklist.md`. Native systems only: no conduction, no core heat, no new resource.

- **Switches** (`animations.json`, applied by Redux's import whatever `enabled` is): `ksp1_radiators`, `radiator_deploy_bridge`, `radiator_auto_on` (default true), `radiator_flux_scale` (default 1, 0.01 to 100), `radiator_ksp1_rates` (default false). Each switch set to `false` gives the behaviour of 0.10.0 for its point.
- **Two conversions.** A fixed radiator becomes `Module_Cooler` (17 installed parts); a radiator with a `ModuleDeployableRadiator` becomes `Module_ActiveRadiator` (15 installed), a `Data_Cooler` plus a `Data_ActiveRadiator`. A part with several radiator modules gets their kW summed. A resource the game does not have is dropped (0 installed).
- **Unit.** kW = `maxEnergyTransfer` / 50, from KSP1's public figures (50000 gives 1000 kW): `radPanelLg` 200 kW, `foldingRadLarge` 1000 kW; the installed set spans 2 to 1000 kW. A larger collection has outliers (Sterling radiators up to millions of kW, HeatControl up to 8000 kW): `radiator_flux_scale` tames them. The balance against the 0.10.0 heat of converters and drills is only checked in game.
- **Electric pace.** EC follows KSP2's native pace, 100 kW per EC/s (read from the game's coolers, fallback 100; line `native: cooler reference:`). A large folding radiator therefore draws about 10 EC/s whenever it is on, where KSP1 drew at most 0.5 EC/s. `radiator_ksp1_rates: true` keeps the cfg rate; other resources (XenonGas) always keep theirs. Each part line logs both EC figures.
- **Deploy bridge.** A deployable radiator cools only while its KSP1 deploy clip is extended, and switches off and hides its toggle when folded, through Redux's own `Module_ActiveRadiator` retract path (the bridge reads the existing Deploy entry; no entry is added and no saved key moves). `radiator_deploy_bridge: false`: it cools folded and is never switched on automatically. The bridge needs three private members of `Module_ActiveRadiator`; a missing one turns it off with one line.
- **Auto-on.** Fixed radiators start on; a deployable one turns on when it is extended (line `thermal: ... radiator turned on when extended`). A radiator the player switched off stays off after a reload: only a later extension turns it on again. A new part is told from a reloaded one by `justLaunched`; if that is missed, the only effect is no auto-on.
- **Start-state repairs.** A save keeps only the cooler switch, and the state comes back from the definition. In flight the mod repairs the mismatch both ways (on with OFF becomes IDLE; off with IDLE becomes OFF, otherwise a switched-off fixed radiator would cool and draw EC while showing off), and again at each extension of a deployable one (lines `thermal: ... cooler state repaired`).
- **Passive radiators** (`emissiveConstant` >= 0.8, no `ModuleActiveRadiator`: 486 installed) already work through Redux's import of `emissiveConstant`; they are only counted (`native: radiators: passive:`).
- **Skipped, and why.** KSP2 cools the vessel through one pool and has no conduction between parts, so `parentCoolingOnly` has no meaning; no core heat (`isCoreRadiator`); `overcoolFactor`; electric use proportional to the load (KSP2's cooler draws at a constant pace); panel sun tracking and wind break (no counterpart). The Heat glow of radiator parts (`ModuleAnimateHeat`) stays dark: KSP2 does not heat a radiator.
- **Audit.** The `native_modules` CSV column carries `Module_Cooler` and `Module_ActiveRadiator`; the `audit: native modules:` line counts `<n> radiator(s)`.
- **Safety.** Each importer failure leaves the part as 0.10.0 imports it. A missing game member turns its feature off with one line. A runtime throw stops the radiator patches for the session (`thermal: patch failed`).
- **Clean room.** No KSP1 or Squad code; Redux is called through its own members, resolved by name, never copied.
- 2147 EditMode tests (2145 passing, 2 explicit and skipped); 2146 before the audit count.

## 0.10.0 - 2026-10-28

Converters, drills and scanners: the KSP1 ISRU modules that Redux dropped are imported as KSP2's own modules. Not run in game yet; checklist (French) in `docs/testing/2026-10-28-converters-drills-in-game-checklist.md`. Native systems only: no new resource, map, economy or heat model.

- **Switches** (`animations.json`, applied by Redux's import whatever `enabled` is): `ksp1_converters`, `ksp1_drills`, `ksp1_survey_scanners`, `ksp1_resource_scanners` (default true), `ksp1_ore_resource` (default `"CarbonRegolith"`), `harvest_multiplier` (default 1, 0.01 to 100), `isru_heat_scale` (default 1, 0 to 10), `isru_active_animation` (default true, needs `enabled`), `isru_ksp1_rates` (default false). Each switch set to `false`, and `ksp1_ore_resource` set to `"TestRocks"` or `""`, gives the behaviour of 0.9.0 for its point.
- **Converters.** The KSP1 `ModuleResourceConverter`s of a part (fuel cells, ISRU units, BDB APUs) become one `Module_ResourceConverter` with one mode per KSP1 converter, chosen in its Mode menu, with readable mode names added to Redux's part localization. Rates go through Redux's own `ConvertAmount`, as the tanks do, so a converter moves exactly what the containers hold; EC is never converted. **Pace ruling:** converters follow the drills' per-EC rule. Each mode that uses EC is scaled by one factor so that it consumes, per EC/s, what KSP2's own converter consumes (median of the KSP2 converter modes that use the same resource, else of all of them; read from the game's parts, else the 26w42c `converter_2v_inline` values). Its EC, heat and input-to-output ratios stay KSP1's. The stock ISRU modes run at about 1/500 of their KSP1 rates (CarbonRegolith 5e-5/s for 30 EC/s), so one KSP1 RadialDrill (about 4.2e-5/s at full density) feeds one ISRU, as KSP2's drill feeds KSP2's converter. Each mode line logs the factor and both per-EC figures (`pace x… : consumes … in KSP1, … in KSP2 (…)`). `isru_ksp1_rates: true` keeps KSP1's rates. Converters without an EC input (fuel cells) keep KSP1's rates. Heat is KSP2's own median heat per EC/s, times `isru_heat_scale`. A mode that uses a resource the game does not have is dropped (counted). Installed set: 18 parts, 24 modes, 0 dropped.
- **Ore remap.** KSP1's Ore becomes `CarbonRegolith` in tanks, converters and drills (`ksp1_ore_resource`): Redux's `TestRocks` has a density map on no body, so a KSP1 drill would mine nothing. The remap is an in-memory entry of Redux's resource table, set at pre-initialization; it is **global**, whatever the campaign feature says: a campaign without the mod, in the same session, also gets CarbonRegolith in its KSP1 Ore tanks. A target the game does not know restores Redux's `TestRocks`.
- **Ore mass.** Redux weighs Ore 0.05 t per unit against KSP1's 0.01 t. In the Ore converter modes the fuel mass made per tonne of Ore is therefore about 5 times lower than in KSP1 (logged as `Ore mass x5` on each mode line); the pace keeps that ratio. The drill-to-converter balance is not KSP1's: drills and converters both follow KSP2 per EC/s (see the pace ruling above).
- **Old saves.** A vessel saved in 0.9.0 with TestRocks in a KSP1 tank gets it back as the remap target on load, mass kept, capped at the capacity (Harmony postfix on `PartComponent.SetState`, lines `ore: old save:` and `ore: old saves: <n> part(s) migrated`). VAB workspaces saved before the remap are **not** migrated: their Ore is lost.
- **Drills.** A KSP1 ground drill (`ModuleResourceHarvester`, `HarvesterType` 0) becomes Redux's `Module_Mine`, with its ground probe from the KSP1 `ImpactTransform` and `ImpactRange` (along the transform's +Z, supposed; signed scale, so a mirrored part keeps the direction) and a deploy state that follows the KSP1 deploy clip. **Yield ruling:** a KSP1 drill yields what KSP2's own drill mines per EC/s, times its EC/s, times its KSP1 `Efficiency` against the stock KSP1 drills' 0.1 per EC/s, times `harvest_multiplier`; so RadialDrill and MiniDrill yield exactly KSP2's drill per EC/s. `harvest_multiplier` replaces `harvest_abundance_at_full_density` (an earlier guess, never released); that old key is reported as unknown. A drill whose probe transform is not found gets no deploy state and stays "not deployed" (line `… the drill stays not deployed (no deploy bridge)`): Redux's ground check would otherwise throw every frame.
- **Minable bodies.** Redux's own density maps, unchanged: CarbonRegolith on Bop, Dres, Duna, Eeloo, Eve, Gilly, Kerbin, Laythe, Moho, Pol, Tylo and Vall; **not** on the Mun, Ike, Drast or Minmus. The minable set is read from KSP2's drills at import: Redux fills its resource maps after the KSP1 import (read in the decompilation), so the `minable resources:` line says `from the KSP2 drill` and no `map on n bodies` line appears.
- **Scanners.** KSP1's `ModuleOrbitalSurveyor` (SurveyScanner) becomes `Module_ResourceScanner` with the KSP2 scanner's resources, power and polar-orbit window and KSP1's scan time (5 s); a `ModuleResourceScanner` with `RequiresUnlock = false` (SurfaceScanner) becomes one for its resource, below its maximum altitude.
- **Active clips.** A `ModuleAnimationGroup`'s running clip (ISRU `ProcessorLarge_running`, `Drill_Running`) plays while the part's KSP2 converter, drill or scanner runs (`isru_active_animation`). No animation entry is added and no saved key moves (tested on the stock parts with the switch on and off).
- **Skipped, and why.** Asteroid and comet drills (`ModuleAsteroidDrill`, `ModuleCometDrill`) and ocean, atmospheric and exospheric harvesters: KSP2 has none of these systems. `ModuleCoreHeat`, `ThermalEfficiency` and `TemperatureModifier`: KSP2 has no core temperature, converters run at full rate. Crew bonuses, `FillAmount`, `DumpExcess` (KSP2 stops a converter when an output is full, KSP1 dumped the excess), `HarvestThreshold`. Scanners with `RequiresUnlock` (BDB magnetometers, OrbitalScanner; 19 installed): KSP2 has no local abundance readout. Several KSP1 converters ran at once; KSP2 runs one mode at a time.
- **Audit.** The `native_modules` CSV column carries `Module_ResourceConverter`, `Module_Mine` and `Module_ResourceScanner`; the `audit: native modules:` line counts converters, drills and scanners.
- **Safety.** Each importer failure rolls the part back to what 0.9.0 imports. A missing game member turns its feature off with one line. Any runtime throw stops every ISRU patch for the session (`isru: patch failed`). A KSP1-imported part carrying a native drill or scanner that the mod did not record logs `isru: <part>: <module> not in our record, runtime left as is`, once per session. A KSP1 harvester with neither an EC input nor `Efficiency` yields KSP2's drill for 1 EC/s (noted).
- **Clean room.** No KSP1 or Squad code; Redux is called through its own members, resolved by name, never copied.
- **Final review fixes.** Old saves now really keep their Ore: Patch Manager's `UpdateSavedVesselPartDefinitions` adds the target container (at its initial units) to the saved part state before `SetState`, so the migration's "saved after the remap" guard skipped every 0.9.0 vessel, silently. A pre-remap save is now told by its saved TestRocks with a container that holds the target and no TestRocks; the target is set (not added) to the converted amount, capped at the capacity. A migrated vessel saves no TestRocks, so it is not migrated twice. KSP1 converters run at KSP2's pace per EC (`isru_ksp1_rates` keeps KSP1's rates; before, a KSP1 ISRU ate about 600 times what a KSP1 drill mines and stopped itself at once). A KSP1 import is recognised by Redux's trailing `KSP1` tag, not by a substring. README: no way back from the Ore remap; checklist: the surface scanner's altitude bound is measured from sea level.
- 2074 EditMode tests (2072 passing, 2 explicit and skipped); 2057 before the final review fixes.

## 0.9.0 - 2026-10-28

B9PartSwitch overrides: what a B9 subtype says beyond its visuals now reaches the KSP2 part. Not run in game yet; checklist (French) in `docs/testing/2026-10-28-b9-overrides-in-game-checklist.md`.

- **Switches** (`animations.json`, all default true, independent of `enabled`): `b9_mass`, `b9_resources`, `b9_module_data`, `b9_module_active` (needs `b9_module_data`), `b9_part_scalars`, `b9_new_part_switches`. `false` restores the 0.8.0 behaviour for that point. Log line `[KSP1PartMod] b9: armed: …`.
- **Mass.** `addedMass` plus `tankMass × volume` is added through a part-switch mass modifier, in the VAB and in flight.
- **Tanks.** The subtype's tank type (`B9_TANK_TYPE`, first definition wins) and RESOURCE nodes replace the base resources and capacities; the first subtype gives Redux's own quantities back. A part with a second tank switch (3 installed, e.g. `bluedog_Gemini_EquipmentModule`) keeps Redux's resources; its mass and part values still apply.
- **Module data.** A subtype's `MODULE` `DATA` block is imported by Redux's own importer and the differing fields are written to the native module through V-SwiFT (engine thrust and Isp, gimbal, RCS, solar panel, command). One transformer per multi-mode engine edits every mode (XLR129; the UA120 S1SRB main mode reads 486.3 kN against 1311.4). Installed raw cfgs: 191 transformers on 79 parts. 33 edits on 7 BDB parts (LR91 main engine and verniers, AJ260 Inline, S4B APS) are skipped because the behaviour appears twice and V-SwiFT edits the first instance only.
- **Modules turned off.** Native off forms for the gimbal (ranges 0), the RCS (no thrust) and the decoupler (not stageable, only when it is the part's only stageable module). Engines are never turned off (zero thrust gives NaN Δv). Modules without a native form stay on, with a log line.
- **Part values.** `maxTemp`, `skinMaxTemp`, `crashTolerance`, `CoMOffset`.
- **New selectors.** Parts that had no part switch but whose B9 subtypes change something get one, with readable names: 28 installed parts gain a `Module_PartSwitch`. The 2b animation gates follow these selectors.
- **Audit.** `b9_overrides` CSV column (appended last), `audit: B9 overrides:` line, and a B9 section in the Markdown report.
- **Not applied.** The part cost (computed and logged; the VAB cost is unchanged), `node`, `TRANSFORM`, `upgradeRequired`, the child's volume choice (the default subtype is used), a second tank switch's resources.
- **Old saves.** A vessel saved in 0.8.0 keeps its 0.8.0 values until it is saved and reloaded in flight; after that the 0.9.0 tank and part values apply, but not the mass delta, until the craft is rebuilt in the VAB.
- **Safety.** One try per part around the whole postfix; each transformer is validated (its `Reverter`, types and values) and a bad one is dropped alone; three unexpected failures stop the feature for the session. Redux's own sets and transformers are never replaced.
- **Clean room.** B9PartSwitch is LGPL: the behaviour comes from its public wiki; no B9 code and no B9 tank data are copied. The volume formula and the dry-mass product are inferred.
- Doc fixes: the `OwnershipPlanner.Plan` comment on `b9AppendSet`, and a README known limit on gates that name a never-appended set.
- **Final review fixes.** A vessel saved in 0.8.0 and not loaded when the game saves had no saved choice for the B9 sets appended after Redux's; Redux filled the gap with the set's first subtype, and the mod now fills it first with the set's default (Harmony prefix on `Data_PartSwitch.GetStoredVariantInformation`, members resolved by name, line `b9: old saves:`). A failure inside the final write of a part keeps the names of the sets it appended. The audit logs once when it cannot read a part's B9 column. README and checklist: part values apply in flight only; turning a `b9_*` key off acts on a vessel in flight from its next save, and its mass delta stays until a rebuild.
- 1907 EditMode tests (1905 passing, 2 explicit and skipped).

## 0.8.0 - 2026-10-28

Native modules, lot 1: the ModuleManager gaps in Redux's patcher, `ModuleToggleCrossfeed` and `ModuleAblator`. Not run in game yet; checklist (French) in `docs/testing/2026-10-28-native-modules-1-in-game-checklist.md`.

- **Switches** (`animations.json`): `mm_plugin_safety_net`, `mm_plugin_folders`, `mm_plugin_folders_auto`, `mm_needs_keep`, `mm_before_after_need_mod`, `mm_arithmetic`, `mm_index_selectors`, `mm_edit_first_match` (default false), `ksp1_crossfeed_toggle`, `ksp1_heatshields`, `ablator_rate_scale` (default 1, 0.01 to 100), `ablator_char`. Each switch set to `false` gives the behaviour of 0.7.0.
- **ModuleManager safety net.** The KSP1 plugins (DLLs) never run under Redux, so the patches written for them are left out: `:NEEDS[X]` is false and `:NEEDS[!X]` true for a curated list (RealFuels, FAR, AJE, Kerbalism, TweakScale...) and for every KSP1 folder that holds a `.dll` and no part, except `mm_needs_keep`; `:FOR`, `:BEFORE`, `:AFTER` patches of those names and the patches inside their folders are dropped. Parts are never removed. The `GameData` folder itself is never blocked.
- **BEFORE/AFTER rule.** As ModuleManager does, a patch whose `:BEFORE[x]` or `:AFTER[x]` names a mod that is not there is left out (a `:FOR[x]` name counts as there; `:LAST` is kept). **Visible change:** some patches that Redux applied in 0.7.0 are dropped. On the installed set, Redux's own `Finalize` run in the editor with these patches drops 122 that pass NEEDS (RemoteTech 73, FerramAerospaceResearch 26, zzRealplume 9, ProceduralFairings 3...), and the in-game `mm: BEFORE/AFTER of an absent mod:` line should give the same (not checked in game). The census of the raw cfg files counts 277 such root patches, but most of them already failed a NEEDS in 0.7.0; 277 is not a count of patches Redux applied. The drops include Bluedog_DB patches that name `zzz_Bluedog_DB` or `zzzBluedog_DB` (5 in the raw files, 4 past NEEDS), which look like upstream typos: ModuleManager never applied them either. `mm_before_after_need_mod: false` restores them.
- **Arithmetic and indices.** `*=`, `+=`, `-=`, `/=` on a single number, and `,n`, `,-n`, `,*` after a value or node name (`@volume,1`, `!MODULE[...],0`, `+MODULE[...],-1`), as ModuleManager does; Redux ignored the operators and edited every match. Installed set: 475 operator lines and 350 indices in the raw cfg files; the in-game log counts only the patches that pass NEEDS, and each edited value or node once per matched part, so its numbers differ (expected from the editor run: 1 arithmetic edit applied, 485 node index edits, 0 value index edits). Census: no index or arithmetic edit touches `animationName`, `animationStateName`, `moduleID`, `shaderProperty` or a module `name`, so no saved animation state is reset.
- **Not reproduced (ModuleManager).** `^=` (regex) and `!=` are counted and left skipped; `#$key$` variables are left as text (counted); a vector-component line with an operator (`@key[1,,] *= x`, 8 installed) is skipped, because Redux would write the operand over every value of the key; `mm_edit_first_match` is off, so an edit without an index still changes every match (the log counts how many matched more than one value, to decide the default).
- **Crossfeed.** `ModuleToggleCrossfeed` (decouplers, adapters) becomes `Module_ToggleCrossfeed`: switch in the VAB and in flight, three action-group actions, starting in `crossfeedStatus` (4 of the 155 installed parts start with crossfeed on). `techRequired` is ignored.
- **Heat shield.** `ModuleAblator` becomes `Module_Heatshield`: it burns the part's Ablator above `ablationTempThresh` and shields the part when its bottom (-Y, supposed) faces the airflow. The burn rate is KSP2's own shield rate (median of the KSP2 shields, logged), scaled by `pyrolysisLossFactor` and `ablator_rate_scale`, and the same for every size. A part without the resource gets no shield. Not reproduced: KSP1's temperature curve (`lossExp`, `lossConst`) and `reentryConductivity`. Known differences: `Module_Heatshield` draws Ablator from the whole vessel, and above 50 MW the flux is compressed as a square root, so large shields outlast small ones at extreme flux (up to 4 times).
- **Char colour.** The colour changer of a shield with `useChar` follows the part's own Ablator (white when full, supposed, to black when empty), without writing to the game (`ablator_char`).
